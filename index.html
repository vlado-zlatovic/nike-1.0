Nike History

Nike’s story began in 1964 as Blue Ribbon Sports, founded by track athlete Phil Knight and coach Bill Bowerman. Operating out of the trunk of Knight’s car, they initially imported low-cost, high-quality running shoes from Japan’s Onitsuka Tiger. Origins & Key Milestones

  • 1971 — The Rebrand & Swoosh: Blue Ribbon Sports split from Onitsuka to make its own shoes. Rebranded as Nike (named after the Greek goddess of victory), graphic design student Carolyn Davidson created the iconic Swoosh logo for just $35.
  • 1972 — The Waffle Sole: Driven to design lighter, grippier footwear, Bowerman famously poured rubber into his wife’s waffle iron, creating Nike's breakthrough Waffle Trainer outsole. That same year, they launched the Cortez, establishing Nike's presence in competitive running.
  • 1979 — Air Cushioning: NASA engineer Frank Rudy introduced encapsulated air technology, launching the Nike Air Tailwind and setting the stage for future cushioning innovations.
  • 1984 — The Jordan Era: Nike signed rookie Michael Jordan, launching the Air Jordan 1 in 1985. The partnership revolutionized sports marketing and cemented sneakers into global youth and street culture.
  • 1987 — Air Max 1: Designer Tinker Hatfield made Nike's Air technology visible for the first time, inspired by the exposed structure of Paris's Centre Pompidou.
  • 1988 — "Just Do It": Wieden+Kennedy launched the famous slogan, transforming Nike from a sports gear producer into a global lifestyle brand.
From a local distributor in Oregon to a multinational giant, Nike built its dominance by combining technical athletic innovation with cultural storytelling. Today, it remains the world's leading supplier of athletic shoes and apparel.

Who We Are

At Nike, we exist to bring inspiration and innovation to every athlete* in the world (*and if you have a body, you are an athlete). We don’t just design footwear and athletic gear—we architect the future of human potential.

Driven by relentless momentum, our culture is rooted in pushing boundaries, breaking barriers, and reimagining what’s possible on and off the field.

What Drives Us

  • Performance Innovation: We merge cutting-edge science with human-centered design to craft high-performance apparel and footwear that empower athletes at every level.
  • Sustainability & Impact: Through our Move to Zero journey, we are actively scaling recycled materials, reducing carbon emissions, and building a zero-waste, circular future.
  • Community & Culture: We harness the universal language of sport to break down inequalities, support grassroots athletic communities, and advocate for positive social change globally.

Looking Ahead

Modern sport is continuously evolving, and so are we. By fusing pioneering digital experiences with sustainable product creation, Nike remains committed to powering the next generation of movement, performance, and expression.
